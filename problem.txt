Fellowship Builder Challenge
Speak to customers. Find a real problem. Build the solution.

This is one challenge with two connected parts. First, speak to real customers of a Mosaic brand and identify a meaningful problem. Look beyond the obvious feedback to understand what is driving the problem and how it fits into the broader customer experience. Then use AI to build a technology-driven solution that addresses it.

Show us your systems thinking, creativity, taste, and understanding of people.

How it works

Both parts are connected and must be submitted together in one application. For Part 1, upload a PDF or Word document with insights from at least five customers. For Part 2, share a working link to your app.

Your research and app must focus on the same Mosaic brand and address the same customer problem. Both parts are required.

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. We are currently hiring for our 2027 Fellowship batch for students graduating in 2027.

Part 1Customer Conversations
Listen to at least five Mosaic customers

Choose a Mosaic brand. Talk to real customers. Tell us what we should do better.

The Problem

Choose one Mosaic brand (for example: Little Joys) and find at least five customers who have used its products. Speak to them about their experience: why they chose the product, how they use it, what they like, where they struggle, and what they wish was better.

Your goal is not just to collect feedback. Look for patterns across the conversations, understand what may be driving them, and identify one customer problem worth solving through technology.

Your Task

Speak to at least five customers of the same Mosaic brand. For each customer, submit their actual name and phone number, the products they have used, what they like, what they do not like, and what more they want us to do. Finish with a short summary of what you learned and what you recommend we improve.

Use these conversations to write one clear problem statement. Explain what you believe is causing the problem or contributing to it and use this as the basis for the technology-driven solution you will build in Part 2.

Answer format: Upload a PDF or Word document covering your customer conversations, key insights and final problem statement.

Rules of the Game

    Speak with real customers who have used the brand’s products; do not invent interviews or responses.
    Ask each customer for permission to share their name, phone number, and feedback with Mosaic for this application.
    Do not decide the problem before conducting the conversations.
    Look for patterns across the conversations instead of treating every comment as a separate problem.
    Choose one meaningful customer problem where technology can address the underlying issue, not just the obvious symptom.

What to Submit

    The Mosaic brand you chose.
    Each customer’s name and phone number.
    The products each customer has used.
    Key insights from each conversation.
    Patterns you noticed across the five conversations.
    What you believe is driving the problem and how it fits into the broader customer experience.
    The different problems you considered.
    The one problem you chose to solve.
    Why you chose this problem over the others.

Both parts are required. Complete Part 1 and Part 2 on the same brand and customer problem before submitting your application.


Build the Solution

Turn your customer insight into a working technology-driven solution.

The Problem

Use the problem statement identified in Part 1.

Your solution should address the underlying customer problem you uncovered, not just one surface-level symptom. It should not be a separate idea chosen before the customer conversations.

Your Task

Use Codex, Claude, or a similar coding agent to build a consumer-facing, technology-driven solution to your chosen problem.

Create a simple, useful experience that a customer could actually use. Focus on solving the problem well instead of adding a large number of features.

Your app could help customers discover, choose, use, track or get more value from a product. These are only examples - build what best addresses the insight you uncovered.

Show us your thinking, creativity and attention to detail through the experience you create.

Answer format: Submit a working browser-based app link and a short explanation of what you built.

Rules of the Game

    Your app must solve the problem identified in Part 1.
    Use Codex, Claude, or a similar coding agent to build it.
    Submit a working link that opens in a web browser.
    Make the main experience easy for us to find and try.
    Do not use customers’ names, phone numbers or other personal information inside the app.
    Submit your own work. AI assistance is encouraged.

What to Submit

    A working link to your app.
    A short explanation of how the app solves the problem.
    Optional: source code, a demo video and the AI tools you used.

Both parts are required. Complete Part 1 and Part 2 on the same brand and customer problem before submitting your application.
